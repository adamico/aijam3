# 2026-05-24: lever-interaction-model

The core idea I keep coming back to: the game plays itself, and I just tune it. Not idle — I'm actively making decisions, but they're configuration decisions, not action decisions. Closest thing I can think of is an autobattler but the "battle" part is TBD. What I do know: there has to be a win/lose condition, otherwise I'm just watching a screensaver. The levers need stakes.
