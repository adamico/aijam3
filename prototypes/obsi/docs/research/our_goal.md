# Project Goals

- **Create a viral game experience**  
  Strive for bold, ambitious design choices that encourage sharing and replayability.

- **Focus on simplicity and ingenuity**  
  Build around a core mechanic that is easy to understand but offers deep gameplay.

- **Prioritize mobile-friendly UX**  
  Design controls and interface for optimal play on mobile devices, while supporting other platforms.

- **Surpass "Kill The Brickman"**  
  Deliver a breakout/shooter roguelike that improves on "Kill The Brickman" by:
  - Removing turn-based mechanics
  - Introducing fast-paced, frenetic gameplay inspired by "Vampire Survivors"
  - Emphasizing real-time action and risk/reward dynamics

- **Actionable Steps**
  1. Prototype the core mechanic with one-button controls.
  2. Test and refine mobile usability and responsiveness.
  3. Implement real-time gameplay loops and remove turn-based elements.
  4. Add roguelike progression and replay incentives.
  5. Playtest for viral potential and iterate based on feedback.

r very final goal is to beat the semi-viral game "Kill The Brickman" at its game: be the breakout/shooter rogue like it should have been by scrapping the turn based mechanics in favor of a frenetic vampire-survivors feel
